FAQs on Casual Wear for Men

What is casual wear for a man?

Casual wear for men includes T-shirts or casual shirts. Sweaters and hoodies are appropriate for colder weather. For bottom wear, jeans or khaki pants are ideal. 

Is A polo shirt casual?

Polo shirts are appropriate for casual wear. They can also be worn as business casuals. Pair your polo shirts with jeans to complete your look.

Is jeans a business casual?

Yes, jeans can be worn for business casual events. While light-washed jeans can be worn as business casual attire, opt for clean dark washed-jeans. Avoid ripped or distressed jeans and bright coloured jeans. Basic and simple jeans that can be layered easily are ideal.

What is not appropriate for business casual?

Ripped jeans, dirty sneakers and flip flops are not appropriate for business casual.  Tank tops, stained T-shirts and graphic T-Shirts which may be inappropriate or offensive should be avoided.

Are joggers casual wear?

Joggers are a comfortable option for casual wear and activewear. They offer convenience and comfort.
