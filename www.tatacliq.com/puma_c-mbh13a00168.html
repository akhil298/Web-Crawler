FAQs on Puma 

1. Is Puma an Indian company?

No, Puma is not an Indian company. It is a globally recognized German multinational corporation that designs and manufactures Puma shoes- athletic and casual shoes, apparel, and accessories.

2. Is Puma owned by Adidas?

No, Puma is not owned by Adidas. While both companies were founded by the Dassler brothers, they are separate entities with distinct ownership.

3. Who is the brand ambassador of Puma India?

The current brand ambassador for Puma India is Indian fast bowler Mohammed Shami.

4. How to know if my Puma is original?

To ensure the authenticity of your Puma product- say your Puma flip flops or Puma white sneakers, look for the distinct Puma logo, check the quality of materials and stitching, and purchase from authorised retailers like Tata CLiQ or the official Puma website.

5. Do Puma sports shoes have all the range for men, women, and kids?
Yes, the brand offers a wide range of Puma sports shoes for men, women, and kids, catering to various preferences and requirements in terms of style, size, and performance.
