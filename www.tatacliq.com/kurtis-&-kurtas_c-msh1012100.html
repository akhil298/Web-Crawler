Kurtis for Women: What You Should Know!

Ladies Kurtis come in all shapes, sizes and colours - that’s the beauty of it! Whether you wear Indian clothes on a daily basis or occasionally, some tips to style kurtis will always be handy.

  • Length: The length of your kurti is an important factor to consider. Looking for something formal? Go for a longer kurta, style it with some ethnic bottoms or palazzos and pair it with some fancy jewellery. If you’re looking to dress-down a long kurta for women, pair it with a pair of matching leggings or jeans! For a younger and modern look you can indulge in some indo-western fashion and pair a short kurti with jeans!
  • Colours: Making a choice between the variety of colours ladies kurtis have to offer can be confusing. Find a colour that suits your skin tone; If you have warm undertones find warm colours like red, mustard yellow, brown, amber, peach or coral. Blues, emerald, lavender and pink look better on cool skin tones.
  • Mix and match: A neutral pair of bottoms goes a long way but don’t be shy of more colourful outfits. The charm of ladies kurtas lies in the beautiful prints and colours. Mix up your ethnic bottoms and kurtis to find a combination that represents you!

FAQs on Kurtis for Women

What is the difference between kurta and kurti?
Kurtas are usually longer and are paired with palazzos, pyjamas or churidars. Kurtis for women are shorter and paired with jeans, leggings and palazzos.

How can I style my kurti?
A kurti can be styled in many different ways depending on your preferences. Ladies kurtis can be paired with palazzos, jeans or churidars. Use Indo-Western fashionable jewellery like Jhumpkis, bangles and necklaces for a fancier look.

How to look slim in a ladies kurta?
Darker colours make you appear slimmer. Opt for dark coloured kurtis, and longer kurtis. Such styles make you appear slimmer in ladies kurtis. Palazzos are another great option as they make you appear slimmer and its loose fit is ideal for comfort.

Can I wear a kurta with jeans?
Kurtas and jeans are a trendy, chic and effortless combination. Pair your cotton kurta with your favourite pair of jeans for a casual look. Long and short kurtis for women can be paired with jeans for a stylish and fashionable look.

Which type of kurti is trending?
Kurtis are a timeless garment, any type of kurti always provides a flattering and elegant look. Some of the most popular and stylish kurtis for women include flare kurtis, straight kurtis, short kurtis and kurtis with palazzos.
