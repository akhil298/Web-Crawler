Best Collection Of Malabar Gold And Diamond Products

One of the growing industries is the jewellery industry, especially among women. Now, in this industry, there is a big name Malabar gold and diamond products, started in 1993 by some enthusiastic entrepreneurs. Which started from a small place has now grown into 10 counties with about 13000 employees and more than 280 stores. Their turnover is also about 4 billion rupees. 

Malabar has made a promise to its customers to give the best services to make their experience cheerful. To top it off, they have a bridal jewellery counter just for the bride and groom-to-be.

Range of Malabar Products 

Malabar not only caters to customers who need heavy jewellery, but also to clients with a taste for light, daily-wear jewellery and accessories at a reasonable price.

Malabar provides rich, quality work at a very convenient price so that anyone can buy it. There is also an assurance of quality, which is very hard to get at such prices.

The gold starts from a minimum of Rs 4000 and can go up to anywhere you want with minimal making charges. There is also a wide range of diamond products available at reasonable prices, ranging from Rs 10000.

Features of Malabar products

Malabar has excelled in its quality, style, and range of jewellery. The features the brand provides are hard to get and that is what Malabar did to stand out in the market. Some of its best features are as follows:

  • Fully insured with a lifetime of maintenance, so there is no worry of theft or damage.
  • Malabar provides a 14 days return policy, for easy returns and refund of purchases. 
  • Complete transparent system of making where the product is made with hallmarked pure gold. 
  • Easy and smooth exchange of products and even a buyback guarantee if you choose to sell your old jewellery in the future.

All these features make Malabar very successful, with the establishment of a good customer relationship and experience, along with a popular jewellery brand.

Some tips on how to wear Malabar gold and diamond products:

Malabar gold and diamond jewellery can easily fit into the category of day-to-day accessories. And if you want something to wear regularly, then the preference should be for some light-weighted chains or maybe bracelets. Some small-sized diamond rings are also cool on a normal day. If you want something for the wedding, then don’t hold back and go for the exclusive bridal jewellery. There, as per your need, you can buy whatever you want, although a bit of a classy and traditional look is advised.

As mentioned above, the best customer relationship and satisfaction are the primary focus for Malabar gold and diamond products. This single point has grown into a culture for the vast franchise. The assurance you get with the product is what the customer needs along with the obviously tested and fully certified jewels. So, now grab your gear and head to the nearest store or its site to explore and buy.
