Woodland Shoes: The Ultimate Adventure Shoe Brand In India

One of the most recognizable shoe brands around the world, Woodland has made a mark with shoe lovers not only in India but across the globe. The manufacturer of some of the most durable and sturdy shoes for men, Woodland has had a presence in India for close to 25 years. Since then, Woodland has positioned itself as an adventure brand, right from the very beginning. It is the most preferred shoe brand by hikers, trekkers, mountaineers, and other outdoor adventure lovers. So, if you are someone who has an adventurous streak and is looking for a pair of shoes that can keep up with that, Woodland is the brand for you. We at TataCLiQ offer you a wide range of Woodland shoes that are must-haves for your footwear collection.

 

What makes Woodland the most loved shoe brand?

First and foremost, the most formidable feature of the Woodland shoes is its durability. Durability is certainly one of the key selling points of Woodland. Even on heavy usage, these shoes can last for 5 to 6 years easily. With a little care, you can look forward to a decade of exploring and adventuring with your Woodland shoe. Woodland shoes are handmade, hand stitched and are very stylish too. They could go very well with trousers, denim, cargo shorts or other casual wear. Woodland has maintained the same quality in their products as it was with the iconic G-0092 which took the market by storm and changed the face of shoe manufacturing forever. One more thing that makes Woodland shoes a class apart from others is the fact that they are committed to the policy of nature first. Woodland shoes are made through processes that cause minimal harm to the environment. Woodland Pro Planet is an initiative by the brand to manufacture greener shoes, hence causing minimal damage to the environment.

 

Does Woodland focus only on outdoor shoes for men?

The answer is no! Not just outdoor shoes, Woodland has a wide range of casual shoes too. Woodland casual shoes offer the comfort of a casual shoe with the same durability which makes Woodland unique in its own way. Woodland sandals such as Woodland khaki fisherman sandals come with a premium quality nubuck leather upper and a sturdy rubber sole and is thus, slip resistant and comfortable as well. Just team it up with a good pair of t-shirt and denim and you are ready to make a great style statement. You can also pamper yourself with a great range of Woodland slippers and flip-flops. They are high on comfort, slip resistant and come with the same Woodland quality which makes them a great buy. You can team them with your casual shorts or jeans. Woodland not only makes a great pair for men but has a wide range of footwear for women as well. Woodland women has a great variety of women footwear such as loafers, sneakers and casual shoes in vibrant colours. These head turner shoes are a must-have in your wardrobe. They keep your feet relaxed and comfortable for a long-time. Ideal not only for the outdoor environment but they are also good for wearing in office. As a reference, Woodland women’s Navy and Tan Moccasins can be worn casually as well as formally. Pair it with a skirt or a trouser and you are ready to slay in your formal attire.

 

What are the best Woodland brand offerings?

One of the best seller for Woodland is their VANS Woodland Camo Old Skool. The original VANS Old Skool when debuted came with its iconic VANS side strip. It was the first skateboarding shoe that was incorporated with leather panels for added durability. This shoe was apparently famous with great skateboarders like Julian Stranger. The Woodland Camo Old Skool is a version of an iconic silhouette of VANS which combines canvas and suede uppers in black, white and camo pallets which makes it amazingly stylish. Apart from style, comfort and performance are the main features this shoe boosts. It has re-enforced toe cap that is able to withstand repeated wear. It has cushioned footbed for that extra support and protection. These unisex shoes are great for outdoors as well as for formal occasions. You can team it with denim, shorts, trousers. Women can also team it up with skirts for that feminine touch and class. Another great shoe in Woodland and VANS camo series is Woodland Camo Slip-On Vans. It elevates your streetwear inspired look. Made for daily comfort, it is a must buy for shoe connoisseurs. Another bestseller for Woodland has to be the DC Woodland boots. Designed for complete warmth and comfort, the DC Woodland boots are your go-to winter boots. It protects your feet in harsh winters, it has a textile lining for breathability and a cushioned insole. Padded collar and tongue provide added comfort. It has high top style with round moctoe. Now you can look forward to that drop-in temperature with the DC Woodland boots.
