FAQs on FabIndia

Why is Fabindia so popular?

FabIndia has a vast collection of ethnic wear for men and women. Their clothes use ethnic prints and designs while remaining modern and fashionable. 

 

Which colour of kurta is best with jeans?

Denim and kurtas are a popular outfit combination. Light coloured kurtas such as light blue, pink and yellow pair best with dark coloured denim jeans. Darker coloured kurtas such as black, indigo and dark purple pair best with light washed jeans.

 

Which saree is soft to wear?

Fabrics such as chiffon, georgette, silk and rayon are soft. Sarees made of these materials are best for formal wear or party wear.

 

What fabric is dupatta made of?

Dupattas can be made of several different materials, however, cotton and chiffon dupattas are the most popular and functional for everyday wear. 

 

What is the fabric used for ethnic wear?

Ethnic wear uses several different fabrics, often native to India. Popular fabrics for ethnic wear are cotton, linen, georgette and silk.
