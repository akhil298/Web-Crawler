FAQs on Utsa Ethnic Wear
What is the difference between kurta and kurti?
Kurtas are longer garments, ending between the knees and ankles. Kurtis usually refer to shorter ethnic tops. 

How can I style my kurti?A kurti can be styled in many different ways depending on your preferences. Ladies kurtis can be paired with palazzos, jeans or churidars. Utsa offers a wide range of palazzos to go with your kurti.

How to look slim in a ladies kurta?
Choose dark coloured kurtis, and longer kurtis as they may make you appear slimmer. Palazzos are another great option as they make you appear slimmer and its loose fit is ideal for comfort.

Can I wear a kurta with jeans?
Kurtas and jeans are a trendy, chic and effortless combination. Long and short utsa kurtis can be paired with jeans for a stylish and fashionable look.

Which type of kurti is trending?
Kurtis are a timeless garment, any type of kurti always provides a flattering and elegant look. Some of the most popular styles include flare kurtis, straight kurtis, short kurtis and kurtis with palazzos.
